PRIVACY / DEVELOPMENT POLICY

Your screen.
Your business.

Annotator is in private testing. The app is free, with optional support through an external page.

Local work

Annotations, desktop pixels and typed content stay on your Mac. Board files contain user-authored drawings, not a continuous screen recording. Explicit PNG export saves the selected output locally.

Free to use

All tools are free. The app does not require an account, activate a license or make license-renewal requests. Optional support does not unlock features.

Optional support payments

The Buy me a beer link opens Buy Me a Coffee only when you click it. That service and its payment providers handle any contribution under their own terms and privacy policies. This website does not collect card details or load a payment widget.

Updates and diagnostics

When a signed update feed is configured, you can check manually or choose automatic checks. Update checks contact the release server; its hosting provider receives the normal connection information, including an IP address. Sparkle system-profile sharing is off.

The app does not currently send usage analytics or crash reports to us. Future usage and crash reporting will require separate choices. We do not plan to collect drawing contents, screen images, clipboard contents, window titles or hardware serial numbers. macOS may handle its own diagnostics according to your system settings.

App errors use local system logs, with error details marked private. Copy support info is a manual action. Hosting providers, collection details and retention periods must be disclosed before remote services are enabled.

This preview website

No checkout, tracking analytics, advertising pixels or email signup are enabled here. Standard hosting logs may exist when deployed; hosting and retention details must be published before the site goes live.

Know the boundary

An overlay cannot guarantee inclusion in another app's capture. Read the masking limitations and prefer sanitized demonstration content when privacy matters.
