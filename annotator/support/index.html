SUPPORT

Tell us what happened.

Report a problem or share feedback on GitHub. You can review the form before submitting it. A GitHub account may be required.

What to include

Include the app version/build, macOS version, display count and the steps that failed. Describe what you expected and what happened. Use fictional content when reproducing a problem.

Public issues can be read by anyone. Remove personal content from screenshots. Never attach credentials, license tokens, private keys or unrelated documents. The app does not submit reports or upload drawings automatically.

Optional support

All tools are free. If you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a beer through Buy Me a Coffee. It's optional and doesn't unlock any features.

The support page opens only when you click the link. There is no embedded payment form on this website.

Protection concerns

Stop sharing first. Keep existing opaque covers in place until the underlying content is safe. See capture and overlay limitations.
