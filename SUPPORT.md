# Getting help

- [Help](https://getannotator.app/docs/) covers first steps, shortcuts, permissions, screen sharing and saving boards.
- The [release notes](https://github.com/dorlugasigal/annotator/releases) list what changed and the known limits of each version.
- [Report a problem](https://github.com/dorlugasigal/annotator/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) if something doesn't work, or [share feedback](https://github.com/dorlugasigal/annotator/issues/new?template=feedback.yml) if something is confusing or missing.

Before you report, update to the latest version. In Annotator, Settings >
About > Copy support info gathers app and system details without drawings or
screenshots. Review it, then paste it into your report. Issues are public, so
use made-up data.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an
issue.
