# Code of conduct

Annotator's issues, pull requests and discussions are public. Keep them
useful and kind.

## Expected

- Be respectful, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic: Annotator's code, docs, problems and ideas.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Posting private information about yourself or anyone else.
- Spam, ads or links to unrelated downloads.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block
accounts that break these rules. To flag something, use **Report content** in
the comment's ⋯ menu, or report it privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/dorlugasigal/annotator/security/advisories/new)
if it involves a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in every project space: the repository, its issues and
pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.
