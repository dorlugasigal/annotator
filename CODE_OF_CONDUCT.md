# Code of conduct

Annotator's issues are public. Keep them useful and kind.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic: problems, feedback and questions about Annotator.
- Don't post private information about yourself or anyone else.
- Don't post spam, ads or links to unrelated downloads.

The maintainer may edit, hide or lock comments and block accounts that break
these rules. To flag a comment, use Report content in the comment's ⋯ menu.
