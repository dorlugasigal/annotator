17:10Almost done

Six things you'll find by Friday.

  1. It fixes wobbly shapes.

    Sketch a rough box, ring, diamond or arrow with the pen. Smart Draw swaps it for a neat one. It's optional, for the days you like the wobble.

  2. It shares one slice of a wide screen.

    Share region puts one part of a display in its own window, and your meeting app shares that window. It exists because sharing a single app can leave your drawings out. One warning: the region can include notifications.

  3. TODO

    It keeps your favourite doodles.

    Got an arrow just right? Save selected drawings or text to My shapes and drop them in again tomorrow.

  4. The toolbar gets out of the way.

    Make it horizontal or vertical, small or large, and add or remove buttons. Leave it alone for a moment and it shrinks to one button.

  5. Your pointer gets a halo.

    Pick its shape, color, opacity and glow, and add an effect for every click. People stop losing your pointer.

18:00Before you install

Straight answers.

Will people on my call see my drawings?
If you share your whole screen, yes. We checked that by hand with Teams on a Mac. If you share one app or one window, the drawings may be left out, so ask someone on the call what they see. Other meeting apps are not checked yet.
Does it record or upload my screen?
No. Annotator is not a screen recorder and never saves video. Your drawings, screen pixels and typed text stay on your computer. Two things do go online: update checks contact a release server, and usage counts, which are off unless you turn them on and never include drawings, screenshots or text.
Is blur safe for secrets?
No. Blur, Pixelate and Distort cover a region for the people watching. Details can stay readable, the cover doesn't follow a window that moves or scrolls, and hiding or quitting Annotator removes it. Take real secrets off the screen before you share.
Is it really free?
Yes. All tools are free. No account. No paid tiers. Coffee is optional, and it unlocks nothing.
Is it the same on Mac and Windows?
It's one product with two native apps. The Windows app matches the Mac app, and the differences are written down. On a Mac it runs from the menu bar, on Windows from the system tray.
Is it finished?
Not yet. The latest release is 0.5.0, and it hasn't reached 1.0. Expect rough edges, and tell us when you hit one.
Does it work on more than one display?
Yes. Each display has its own drawings, screen zoom starts on the display under your pointer, and the break timer can cover all of them.
What permissions does it ask for?
Drawing needs none. Screen zoom, live effects and Share region need screen access: Screen Recording on a Mac, the screenshots and screen recording setting on Windows.
Can I keep what I drew?
Yes. Save a board and reopen it later, still editable, or export it as a PNG.
I already use Control-Shift-2 for something else.
Change it. Every key can be changed in Settings.
