All the tools

Every tool, by name.

Everything in the tool library, group by group. All tools are free. The number after a name is its key while you draw.

Eight of them at work.

Each clip is a recording of the real app, on a sample dashboard.

  1. Ellipse

    Choose Ellipse and circle a number.

  2. Arrow

    Drag for a straight arrow, or click to place its points. Double-click it to add a point, then drag that point to bend it.

  3. Smart Draw

    Sketch a loop or an arrow, and Smart Draw makes it neat.

  4. Highlighter

    Drag it across a line of text.

  5. Spotlight

    The screen dims around your pointer.

  6. Blur

    Drag over a busy picture to cover it.

  7. Screen zoom

    Zoom in and keep talking.

  8. Tool library

    Pick a shape or a diagram icon.
