How it works

Three things to know.

Press the keys now. They work on this page.

  1. One shortcut puts you on the layer.

    Pick a tool and draw. Your marks land on the layer, not in your document.

  2. Esc gives you your app back.

    Click, type and scroll as usual. What you drew stays on screen.

  3. Nothing you draw is final.

    Move a mark, bend an arrow, change its color, lock it, undo it. Every mark stays editable.

You are using your apps. Drawings stay visible.

⌃2Ctrl+2 Hide or show everything ⌃1Ctrl+1 Zoom ⌃3Ctrl+3 Break timer ⌘LCtrl+L Tool library Change any of them in Settings.

Also on the layer

Things you don't expect.

  1. Smart Draw

    Your wobbly loop comes out clean.

    Turn on Smart Draw for the pen. It recognises a hand-drawn loop or arrow and makes it neat, so drawing with a mouse stops being embarrassing.

  2. Share region

    Share one corner of a very wide screen.

    Share region puts one part of a display in its own window, and your meeting app shares that window. It is also the fix when sharing a single app leaves your drawings out of the call.

  3. Break timer · ⌃3Ctrl+3

    “Back in ten”, and the screen counts for you.

    A full-screen countdown, anywhere from 1 to 180 minutes, with a title and a chime. It starts as a 10-minute Coffee break.

    Coffee break10:00

  4. Whiteboard · Blackboard

    A clean board when the screen is too busy.

    Swap the desktop on one display for a whiteboard or a blackboard, draw, and swap back. Save a board and it reopens later with every mark still editable.

  5. My shapes

    Draw your logo once.

    Select any drawing or text and save it to My shapes. Next time it is one click away.

  6. Cursor halo

    A pointer people can actually find.

    Give your pointer a halo and choose its shape, color, opacity and glow. Add a click effect so viewers see when you press.

  7. AI control

    An AI assistant can hold the pen.

    An optional skill teaches an AI app that already has computer-use tools to use Annotator the way you do: pick a tool, draw, edit, check. It adds no permissions of its own. The AI provider may receive screenshots under its own privacy settings. How AI control works

Straight answers

What never happens to your screen.

Does it record or upload my screen?

No.

  • It isn't a recorder. Annotator does not record or save video.
  • Nothing leaves. Drawings, screen pixels and typed text stay on your computer.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign in to.
  • Permissions only when needed. Drawing needs none. Screen zoom, live effects and Share region ask for screen access.
  • What does go out. Update checks contact a release server. Usage counts are off unless you turn them on, and they never include drawings, screenshots or text. Privacy page
Will people on my call see my drawings?
If you share your whole screen, yes. If you share one app or one window, the drawings may be left out, so ask someone on the call what they see. Share region is made for that case.
Is blur safe for secrets?
No. Blur, Pixelate and Distort cover a region live, but details can stay readable. The cover doesn't follow a window that moves or scrolls, and it goes away when you hide or quit Annotator. Take real secrets off the screen first.
Is it really free?
Yes. All tools are free. No account. No paid tiers. Coffee is optional, and it unlocks nothing.
Is it the same on Mac and Windows?
It is one product with two native apps. The Windows app matches the Mac app, and where they differ, the difference is written down.
Is it finished?
Not yet. The latest release is 0.5.0 and it hasn't reached 1.0, so expect rough edges. Tell us when you find one.
Does it work with more than one display?
Yes. Each display has its own drawings.
Can I keep what I drew?
Save a board and reopen it later, still editable, or export it as a PNG.
What happens when I hide it?
The drawings and the toolbar go away. Show them again and the drawings are back.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Or with Homebrew brew install --cask dorlugasigal/annotator/annotator
  1. 1

    Download and open the disk image.

  2. 2

    Drag Annotator to Applications.

  3. 3

    Open it. Pick a tool. Draw. It runs from the menu bar and updates itself.
