Also in the app

More than 40 tools, and a few things you might not expect.

  • Share region

    Put one part of your screen in its own window, so a meeting app can share just that.

  • Break timer

    A full-screen countdown for the break. It starts as a 10-minute coffee break.

  • Whiteboard and blackboard

    Swap the desktop for a clean board, then switch back.

  • Saved shapes

    Save a drawing you use often and drop it in again.

  • Your toolbar

    Drag tools in or out, or shrink it to one button.

  • Cursor halo

    Give your pointer a color, a glow and a click effect.

Good to know

Will people on my call see it?

Share your whole screen and they see your drawings. Sharing one app or window may leave them out, so ask someone on the call what they see.

Does it record my screen?

No. Annotator is not a screen recorder. Your drawings, your screen and what you type stay on your computer.

Is it really free?

Yes. All tools are free. No paid tiers, and the code is open source under Apache 2.0.

Is it the same on Mac and Windows?

Yes. Two native apps, one product. It is not at 1.0 yet, so expect a few rough edges.
