Rendering check

Each scene is one of the app’s pictures (reference/conformance/render, drawn on a Mac) beside the same board drawn by the web demo’s renderer (scene.js), at 1 and 2 pixels per point. Around every element the demo draws, each pixel must lie within the range the other picture shows within one pixel, plus 32/255 for antialiasing: the edge rule of the app’s own rendering tolerance (docs/parity.md, WIN-REND-05). At most 1 pixel in 100 of an element’s ink may break it, to allow for two different rasterizers. Elements the demo does not draw (fills, diagram shapes, ink pressure, other arrowheads) are left out and counted.
