Details

Keys
CtrlShift2 to draw. Esc to go back to your app. Your drawings stay.
Privacy
Your drawings and your screen stay on your computer.
Price
All tools are free, and the source is open under Apache 2.0.

Install

macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Runs from the menu bar.

Download for Mac

Or with Homebrew

brew install --cask dorlugasigal/annotator/annotator

Windows 11, version 22H2 or later, x64 and ARM64. Runs from the system tray.

Get it from Microsoft Store

Or with winget

winget install --id 9NMZ345ZJ6JK --source msstore
