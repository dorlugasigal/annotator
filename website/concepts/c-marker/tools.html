Draw

The ones you reach for first. Each has a key.

  • Select

    Move, resize and restyle a mark. Select several at once, or lock the ones that must stay.

    1
  • Pen

    Draw freehand. With Smart Draw on, hand-drawn loops and arrows come out neat.

    7
  • Highlighter

    Hold Shift for a straight line.

    9
  • Laser pointer

    The trail fades on its own. You choose how fast, from 0.5 to 5 seconds.

    L
  • Text

    Type a label on the screen.

    8
  • Arrow

    Drag for a straight one, or click to place it point by point. Double-click a selected arrow to add a point, then drag the point to bend it.

    5
  • Line

    6
  • Eraser

    Removes unlocked marks. Undo brings them back.

    0

Focus

For the small part of the screen that nobody at the back can read.

  • Spotlight

    Dims the screen around your pointer.

  • Cursor halo

    Makes your pointer easier to spot. Choose its shape, color, opacity, glow and click effect.

  • Pointer lens

    A magnifier at the pointer.

  • Screen zoom

    Zooms in around the pointer and holds still while you work. A zoom map shows the whole display and where you are.

    ⌃1Ctrl+1
  • Share region

    Puts one region of one display in its own window, so a meeting app can share that window. Anything inside the region shows up, including notifications.

Effects

Live covers for a region of the screen. They are not secure redaction: details can stay readable, and a cover doesn't follow a window that moves or a page that scrolls. Remove private details before you share.

  • Blur

    Covers a region live.

  • Pixelate

    Covers a region live.

  • Distort

    Covers a region live.

  • Censor

    A solid cover.
