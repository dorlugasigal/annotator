Good to know

Will people on my call see it?
They will if you share your whole screen. If you share one app or window, the drawings may be left out, so ask someone what they see. Share region is made for that case.
Does it record my screen?
It doesn't. Annotator is not a recorder, and your drawings, your screen and what you type stay on your computer. Drawing needs no permissions; zoom and live effects ask for screen access.
Is it really free?
All tools are free. No account. No paid tiers. The code is open source under Apache 2.0.
Is it the same on Mac and Windows?
Both are native apps, built as one product so they match. Annotator is still before version 1.0, so expect a few rough edges.
