What you can do

Zoom in

Zoom in where your pointer is, so the small stuff is easy to read. A little map shows where you are.

Shapes and icons

More than 40 tools, with shapes and icons for servers, databases and the rest.

Share just one part

Put one area of a wide screen in its own window, then share that window.

Take a break

A big countdown on the screen, so everyone knows when to come back.

Good to know

Will people on my call see my drawings?
Yes, if you share your whole screen. If you share a single window they might not, so ask someone what they see.
Does it record my screen?
No. Your drawings, your screen and what you type stay on your computer.
Is it really free?
Yes. All tools are free and the code is open source. If you like it, there's a coffee button.

Get Annotator

Mac

macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. It lives in your menu bar.

Download for Mac

Or use Homebrew:

brew install --cask dorlugasigal/annotator/annotator

Windows

Windows 11, version 22H2 or later. It lives in the system tray, near the clock.

Get it for Windows

Or use winget:

winget install --id 9NMZ345ZJ6JK --source msstore
