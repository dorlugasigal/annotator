Draw on your screen as you explain.

Annotator is a free app for Mac and Windows. Circle a number, point with an arrow, blur what is private. Everything you draw stays editable.

A recording of the app

Two keys

ControlCtrlShift2 to draw. Esc to go back to your app. Your drawings stay.

Private

Your drawings and your screen stay on your computer. Drawing needs no permissions.

Free

All tools are free. No paid tiers. Open source under Apache 2.0.

Install

Mac

macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Runs from the menu bar.

Download for Mac

Or with Homebrew

brew install --cask dorlugasigal/annotator/annotator

Windows

Windows 11, version 22H2 or later, x64 and ARM64. Runs from the system tray.

Get it from the Microsoft Store

Or with winget

winget install --id 9NMZ345ZJ6JK --source msstore
